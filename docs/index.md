# Law Firm Website Video Planner

Which videos a law firm website actually needs, where each one goes, how long it should be, and how to add video without slowing the site down or creating an advertising-rule problem. Published by [Legal Web Agency](https://legalwebagency.com/).

Video helps a prospective client hear the attorney's voice before they call. That is its real job on a law firm site: to build enough trust that picking up the phone feels easy. It does not need to be cinematic. For the bigger picture, read our guides to [law firm website video](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-website-video/) and [video marketing for lawyers](https://legalwebagency.com/video-marketing-for-lawyers/).

## The core set: five videos

| Video | Where it goes | Length | Purpose |
|-------|---------------|--------|---------|
| Firm introduction | Homepage, below the main headline | 60 to 90 seconds | Who you help, where, and what working with you is like |
| Attorney introduction | Each attorney bio | 45 to 60 seconds | A face and voice for the name. See [attorney bio page content](https://legalwebagency.com/attorney-bio-page-content/) |
| Practice explainer | Each main practice page | 1 to 3 minutes | What happens in this type of case, step by step |
| FAQ answers | Practice pages and the FAQ page | 30 to 60 seconds each | One question per clip. See [law firm website FAQs](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-website-faqs/) |
| What to expect at a consultation | Contact page | 45 seconds | Lowers the fear of the first call. See [law firm contact page design](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-contact-page-design/) |

Start with the firm introduction and one practice explainer. Add the rest over a few months.

## Recording without a studio

- Use a phone on a tripod, at eye level, in landscape for the website.
- Face a window for soft light; avoid overhead lights.
- Use a clip-on microphone. Bad audio loses viewers faster than bad video.
- Speak to one imagined client, not "viewers."
- Write bullet points, not a full script, so it sounds natural.
- Add captions. Many people watch with the sound off.

## Keeping the site fast

Video is one of the most common reasons law firm pages load slowly on phones.

1. Host on YouTube or Vimeo, not on your own server.
2. Load a preview image first and load the player only when someone clicks play.
3. Never autoplay a background video on mobile.
4. Keep only one video near the top of any page.

## Staying within advertising rules

- No promises about outcomes, and add a results disclaimer if you mention verdicts or settlements. See [the case results page guide](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-case-results-page/).
- Label actors and dramatizations clearly.
- Get written consent before featuring a client.
- Avoid *expert* and *specialist* unless your state allows it.

Rules differ by state; see the [attorney advertising rules by state](https://legalwebagency.com/attorney-advertising-rules-by-state-law-firm-website/) hub.

## Helping videos get found

- Give each video a descriptive title with the practice and city.
- Put a short summary or transcript on the page under the video.
- Add VideoObject structured data. See [schema markup for law firms](https://legalwebagency.com/schema-markup-for-law-firms/).
- Add videos to the firm's Google Business Profile too. See [Google Business Profile for law firms](https://legalwebagency.com/google-business-profile-for-law-firms/).
